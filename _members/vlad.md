 I am an undergraduate researcher studying Mechanical Engineering at FAU, currently working in the SCUBA Lab. My work involves developing an autonomous ground robot and gradually integrating additional sensors to enhance its capabilities. I conduct experiments in a controlled environment using the OptiTrack motion capture system, which serves as a reliable platform for validating robot behavior. This research helps demonstrate how motion capture systems can be used to test and refine autonomous navigation in real-world scenarios.
