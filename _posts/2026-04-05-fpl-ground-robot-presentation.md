SCUBA Lab recently delivered a successful presentation to FPL on our ground robot system for object detection and tracking.

The platform combines a stereo vision camera and a thermal camera to improve perception and enable robust target detection and tracking across challenging operating conditions.

We appreciate the opportunity to share this work and discuss its potential for field deployment and future collaboration.
