Taiwo Hazeez, Cosme Penney and Daniel Guido had their paper titled "Evaluating Relative Localization in GPS-Denied Environments Using Starling2 UAVs" accepted!

Daniel Granda, Vladimir Gunichev and Caleb Sanchez had their paper titled " Accelerating Early-Stage Robot Navigation Development with Motion Capture and ROS" accepted!

Caleb Sanchez had his abstract "Revealing All the Mysteries of Using Motion Capture System for Robot Localization" accepted!

Congrats to all the SCUBA roboticists!
